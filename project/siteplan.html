Site Plan

Site Name

Name: Pacana Punta Cana

Logo: 🌴🏖️ (temporary logo)

Description: I chose this name because it reflects the tropical and relaxing atmosphere of Punta Cana, making it attractive for visitors looking for unique Caribbean experiences.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to provide information about cultural tours, nature experiences, and local events in Punta Cana. It will serve as a hub for visitors to explore activities, book tours, and connect with the community.

Scenarios

  • What cultural and nature tours can I book through Pacana Punta Cana?
  • Where can I find information about upcoming local events and community activities in Punta Cana?

Color Scheme

  • #264653 – Primary text color
  • #2a9d8f – Buttons and links
  • #e9c46a – Accent highlights
  • #ffffff – Background

Typography

Heading Font: Poppins – used for titles and headings.

Body Font: Roboto – used for paragraphs and general text.
